ABOUT US

A place to learn, grow and belong.

Kiana’s Promise Learning Academy provides individualized and small-group academic support for elementary and middle school students. We complement classroom instruction with patient, structured learning and measurable progress.

OUR MISSION

Education with a greater purpose.

Our mission is to empower every child to reach their fullest academic potential through personalized instruction, accessible educational support and meaningful community investment. A child's opportunity to succeed should not be limited by their family's financial circumstances.

Every paid tutoring session brings us closer to sponsoring a child in need. We plan to allocate a percentage of paid session revenue toward sponsored tutoring.

OUR COMMUNITY

Bridging the gap in Rock Hill.

Families may need additional individualized academic support beyond the school day. Our academy aims to complement local educators and expand access to consistent tutoring, helping students build confidence and strengthen academic foundations.
